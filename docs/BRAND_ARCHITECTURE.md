# Brand Architecture

## Recommended hierarchy

### CannaCore Seeds
The company/genetics identity. Owns the commercial relationship, strain collection, breeder releases, and `cannacoreseeds.com`.

### JJ Stash
The editorial/media identity. It is explicitly labeled **JJ Stash — a CannaCore publication**.

This lets JJ Stash grow into a recognizable personality and audience asset without forcing an immediate rebrand away from a domain and seed identity that already exist.

## Why this is preferable to replacing CannaCore with JJ Stash

1. The names have different jobs. CannaCore sounds like a genetics house; JJ Stash sounds like a publication/community identity.
2. The Beehiiv newsletter already describes itself around cannabis genetics, cultivation, history, and culture, which naturally supports the genetics company.
3. If JJ Stash becomes much larger later, it can become an endorsed media brand or independent publication without breaking CannaCore customer recognition.

## Naming system

Preferred public usages:

- **CannaCore Seeds**
- **JJ Stash — a CannaCore publication**
- **From the Stash** for editorial modules on product/genetics pages

Avoid presenting JJ Stash as a second seed bank on the same site.
